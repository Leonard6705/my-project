What to Expect in Brunei

Brunei is an Islamic country and the people here are very respectful toward each others. They are also taught to forgive each other.

Friday Prayers

Friday is a prayer day for all muslims and they are expected to gofrom 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM. During this time, businesses temporarily close to show respect.

Halal Country

While most shops do not sell pork due to it being haram, it is not banned. As you can still find it sold in Chinese shops.

House Etiquette

When visiting you are expected to take off your shoes outside and shake the men hand to show appreciation for welcoming you into their house.

Local Cuisine

Brunei does made its own and below are a few of the popular dish that can be found and usaully eat by the local there.

Ambuyat

The national dish of Brunei made out of sticky starch and it usally come side dish to pair it with like flavor to eat.

Nasi Katok

Brunei most famous dish eat by local everyday and can be found usally within 1 BND which explain the popularity since it come with chicken, sambal and rice.

Kuih

This is mainly eaten as dessert and it come in different varieties such as kuih cincin, tapai, penyaram and more. Learn more
